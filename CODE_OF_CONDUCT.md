# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We want participation in Agent Regression Kit to be safe, constructive and
welcoming. Be respectful, discuss technical evidence rather than people, accept
good-faith correction, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual
attention, or publication of another person's private information.

我们希望所有参与者都能安全、建设性地协作。请尊重他人，讨论技术证据而不是攻击
个人，接受善意纠正；禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、不受欢迎的性关注以及公开他人隐私。

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior puts the community at risk.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting
channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

维护者可以删除不当内容，并在行为危害社区时临时或永久限制参与。行为问题请通过
仓库的私密安全报告渠道反馈，不要在公开 Issue 中提交敏感个人信息。
